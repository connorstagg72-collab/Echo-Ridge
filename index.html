Layout

Three bedrooms, plus a fourth for the kids.

Seven beds and two queen sofa-beds across two levels — enough room for two families, a team, or a reunion that would not fit anywhere else. Here is the walk-through, floor by floor.

Lower level

Vaulted living room, full kitchen, covered deck

  • Master bedroom — queen bed and a playpen. The walk-in closet holds sofa-bed bedding, iron and board, steamer, high chair, a children's collapsible table, and extra seating.
  • Adjoining bedroom — two twin-over-full bunk beds, smart TV, and sports equipment.
  • Bathroom — tub-shower, large towels, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, makeup remover cloths, hair dryer, plenty of toilet paper.
  • Front closet — big pool towels, pool noodles, and shoe storage.
  • Living room — vaulted ceiling, fireplace, pull-out queen sofa-bed and a smart TV.
  • Dining room and full kitchen — everything you need to cook for the whole group.
  • Laundry room off the kitchen, with laundry pods and cleaning supplies provided.
  • Covered deck looking out over the lawn, playground and clubhouse.
Upper level

A loft retreat and the garden-tub bath

  • Loft retreat — a second living room with sectional recliners, open to the vaulted ceiling below.
  • Pull-out queen sofa-bed in the upstairs sitting area.
  • Master bedroom — queen bed, office desk and chair, smart TV.
  • Adjoining closet-bedroom — full bed, best suited to children; also stores the bedding for the upstairs sofa-bed.
  • Ensuite bathroom — double sinks, a garden tub, and a separate shower and toilet.
  • Interior balcony overlooking the living room below.
Parking

Plenty of it, trailers included

  • Plenty of parking throughout the resort — no circling for a spot.
  • Trailer parking available, so bring the boat or the side-by-sides.
